# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

POA Estudio serves high-value private clients, international property owners, investors, and developers planning architecture, renovation, interior design, or residential development work in Spain. Visitors need to understand the studio's design quality, technical control, geographic reach, and ability to manage projects at a distance before deciding to make contact.

## Product Purpose

The website presents POA Estudio's complete bilingual body of work, expertise, team, and working method. Its primary purpose is to build confidence through architecture and evidence, help visitors find relevant projects and services quickly, and generate qualified project enquiries.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, exacting, assured. The voice is concise, cultured, technically credible, and calm. Luxury is communicated through proportion, material, photography, and precision rather than decorative signals or promotional language.

## Anti-references

- Generic luxury architecture templates, especially black-and-gold styling or ornamental serif clichés.
- Raw SEO landing-page structures, keyword headings, duplicated claims, and imported CMS utility text.
- Oversized logo treatments or repeated brand marks used as decoration.
- Small eyebrow headings, numbered section labels, stamps, badges, and images embedded inside titles.
- Uneven masonry layouts, arbitrary empty grid cells, and card collections without a clear information purpose.
- Icon-led buttons, paired generic hero buttons, and arrows used as decoration inside calls to action.
- Dense accordions with clipped inactive labels, dark imagery, or excessive copy revealed on hover.
- Overlong pages that repeat services, contact information, and positioning statements.

## Design Principles

1. Let the built work lead. Photography and project evidence should carry more authority than promotional copy.
2. Edit before adding. Every section must have a distinct purpose, and duplicated CMS or SEO content should be removed from presentation.
3. Make structure feel effortless. Bilingual navigation, project discovery, services, and contact paths should remain obvious without visual noise.
4. Express luxury through precision. Typography, spacing, image treatment, and motion must feel controlled, deliberate, and durable.
5. Use motion to clarify relationships. Transitions should support spatial continuity and interaction, never conceal labels or compete with the work.
6. Keep the shape language rounded and systematic. Use small radii for nested media, medium radii for cards, large radii for grouped modules, extra-large radii for architectural sections, and full pills for actions; viewport overlays may remain edge-to-edge.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA for contrast, semantics, keyboard access, focus visibility, and responsive readability. Maintain the designed motion experience without a separate reduced-motion variant. Motion must remain controlled, avoid flashing or disorienting continuous movement, and never be required to understand content or complete navigation.
